Training data notes (amyloid-predict)

This folder's training scripts read precomputed embedding tables from:
- model_development/features/*.csv
and labels from:
- model_development/labels/*.csv

Sequence availability in this repo:
- WALTZall_labels.csv: includes Sequence column
- WALTZtht_labels.csv: includes Sequence column
- TANGO_Table2_labels.csv: includes Amino Acid Sequence column
- tau_labels.csv: contains fragment position IDs and labels (not raw fragment sequences)

If regenerating embeddings from sequences:
1) Prepare FASTA entries matching label row identifiers/order.
2) Use ESM extraction script from the ESM repo, e.g.:
   https://github.com/facebookresearch/esm
   script: scripts/extract.py
3) Export one embedding row per sequence/fragment and save as CSV in model_development/features/.
